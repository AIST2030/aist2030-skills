## 组合示例

**环绕（默认推荐）**
> `[Product] 360° smooth orbit rotation, camera circles around the subject, cinematic lighting, clean dark background, keyframe animation smooth transition between multiple angles`

**微距 + 环绕**
> `[Product] extreme macro close-up with 360° orbit, ultra-detailed texture, soft studio lighting, pure black background, smooth keyframe rotation`

**慢动作**
> `[Product] slow motion elegant 360° rotation, fluid graceful movement, cinematic lighting, premium commercial style, keyframe animation`

## 通用风格后缀（附加到任意运镜片段后）

- 商业质感：`cinematic lighting, premium commercial style, studio quality`
- 纯黑背景：`clean black background, isolated product, no distractions`
- 柔和光影：`soft diffused studio lighting, gentle highlights`
- 硬朗科技感：`sharp lighting, high contrast, tech product showcase`
- 自然温暖：`warm natural lighting, lifestyle aesthetic, soft shadows`
